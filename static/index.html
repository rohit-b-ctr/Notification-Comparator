➕ Paste payload(s) as golden

Some ISD payloads (large PICK/audit samples) can't be auto-extracted because the PDF's JSON is malformed (smart quotes, line-wrapped tokens). Copy a payload from the doc, paste it here, click ✨ Beautify to validate/fix, then save. Accepts one object, an array, or several pasted objects — plain ISD payloads, or a raw Kowl message/envelope (partitionID/offset/value.payload) copied straight from a Kowl capture.
